uAI-NEXUS-MedVLM (MedGRPO) Assess

Vision-language model for heterogeneous medical and surgical video understanding, built on Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct and trained with supervised fine-tuning followed by MedGRPO — a Group Relative Policy Optimization variant introducing cross-dataset reward normalization (mapping each dataset's median performance to a common reward) and a medical LLM judge that scores caption quality across five clinical dimensions. Trained on the MedVidBench Standard split (51,505 balanced video-instruction pairs across 8 surgical-video sources: AVOS, CholecT50, CholecTrack20, Cholec80-CVS, CoPESD, EgoSurgery, JIGSAWS, NurViD). Targets temporal action localization, spatiotemporal grounding, video summarization, region captioning, and surgical skill / Critical View of Safety (CVS) assessment. On the authors' MedVidBench benchmark the SFT baseline reportedly outperforms GPT-4.1 and Gemini-2.5-Flash across all tasks, with MedGRPO further improving grounding and captioning. Released as an alpha (v1.0a). Northeastern University and United Imaging Intelligence; CVPR 2026.

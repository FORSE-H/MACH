TRIDENT Assess

Large-scale whole slide image processing toolkit from the Mahmood Lab at Harvard Medical School. Provides a single-command pipeline for tissue segmentation, patch coordinate extraction, and patch or slide-level feature embeddings. Integrates 22+ patch encoders (UNI, CONCH, Virchow, H-optimus, GigaPath, and others) and 6 slide encoders (TITAN, PRISM, CHIEF, Madeleine, Feather) via a unified interface. Supports OpenSlide, cuCIM, OME-Zarr, and Zeiss CZI readers with multi-GPU support. Designed as the standard preprocessing pipeline feeding downstream MIL classifiers; backwards-compatible with CLAM patch coordinates. CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0, 553 stars, v0.3.0 (May 2026). arXiv 2502.06750.

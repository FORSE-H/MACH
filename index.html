Editorial judgement

Curated, not
just aggregated

Each entry carries an editorial rating with a public, evidence-backed rationale. Borrowed from the ThoughtWorks Tech Radar, adapted for open healthcare tooling.

Adopt

Production-ready, well-governed, recommended for new work.

Situational

Right in specific contexts. Requires deliberate choice.

Assess

Promising but not yet proven at production scale.

Caution

Avoid for new work — superseded or unmaintained.

How it works

Git-native,
PR-driven curation

All catalog data lives as JSON-LD files in the repository. Adding an entry is a pull request. CI validates every submission automatically.

Step Action Who
01 Copy a template from entries/software/ or entries/models/ Contributor
02 Fill in required fields including judgement and evidence links Contributor
03 CI validates JSON-LD schema, required fields, and duplicate identifiers Automated
04 Editor reviews judgement rationale and evidence quality Curator
05 Merge → catalog exports regenerated → site updated Automated

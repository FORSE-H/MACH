Prov-GigaPath Adopt

Whole-slide pathology foundation model trained on real-world clinical data from Providence Health System — the first WSI-level foundation model with a dual-component architecture: a tile encoder for local patch features and a slide encoder for global slide-level representations. Trained on a large proprietary dataset of H&E slides from Providence clinical archives. Evaluated on cancer subtyping, survival prediction, and mutation detection across TCGA. Published in Nature 2024. Apache 2.0, 46k+ monthly HuggingFace downloads. Developed jointly by Providence Healthcare and Microsoft Research.

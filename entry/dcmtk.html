DCMTK Adopt

Reference C/C++ implementation of the DICOM standard — a comprehensive collection of libraries and command-line tools covering DICOM data encoding and decoding, image processing, networking (DIMSE, DICOMweb), compression, digital signatures, structured reporting, and radiation therapy data. Developed and maintained by OFFIS e.V. (Germany) since the 1990s. Ships 23 specialised sub-packages. Used as the foundation for many commercial and open-source PACS, modality, and imaging middleware systems.

CONCH Assess

Vision-language foundation model for histopathology pretrained on 1.17 million image-caption pairs from PubMed Central open-access articles (H&E, IHC, and special stain images). Contrastive learning aligns visual and textual representations, enabling zero-shot classification, image-text retrieval, and captioning. ViT-B/16 vision encoder (90M params) + 110M text encoder. State-of-the-art across 14 computational pathology tasks. Notably performs well on non-H&E stains (IHC, special stains). Published in Nature Medicine 2024. CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0, 86k+ monthly HuggingFace downloads. Mahmood Lab, Harvard/BWH.

SynthCity Assess

Open-source Python library for generating and evaluating synthetic tabular data across multiple modalities — tabular, time series, survival analysis, and images. Pluginable architecture with 20+ generative models including GAN-based (CTGAN, PATEGAN, DP-GAN, SurvivalGAN), VAE-based (TVAE, SurvAE), diffusion (DDPM), LLM-based (GReaT), and time-series methods (TimeGAN, TimeVAE). Includes comprehensive evaluation metrics for privacy, fidelity, and utility. Directly applicable to clinical trial data augmentation, privacy-preserving dataset sharing, and synthetic EHR generation. NeurIPS 2023 paper. pip-installable. 664 stars. Apache-2.0. van der Schaar Lab, University of Cambridge.

Independent · optional · the catalog stays free

Hands-on help with open-source healthcare software

The MACH catalog is free and open, and always will be. Separately, I offer paid consultancy, auditing, and proof-of-concept implementation for teams that want expert help putting open-source healthcare tools into practice.

To be clear: the catalog, its data, and its judgements are free, open, and independent — nothing here gates them. These services are optional expert help from the person who built MACH, for organisations that want a deeper, hands-on engagement.

What I offer

Focused, practical engagements around open-source healthcare software, models, and standards.

Advisory

Consultancy & tool selection

Deciding which open-source tool fits your context — imaging, FHIR/interoperability, clinical NLP, data platforms, AI tooling — and how it compares to alternatives.

  • Landscape & shortlist for a specific need, with the evidence behind each option
  • Build-vs-adopt guidance and open-vs-commercial trade-offs
  • Standards fit — FHIR, DICOM, OMOP, openEHR and what adopting them really involves

Assurance

Auditing & due diligence

An independent, transparent assessment of an open-source tool or model before you rely on it — openness, maintenance, licence, provenance, and fit for your setting.

  • Openness & licence audit — is it genuinely open, and usable in your context?
  • Maintenance & health review — activity, governance, longevity risk
  • Provenance & archival — reproducibility, Software Heritage archiving, citability

Delivery

Proof-of-concept implementation

A working proof of concept so you can evaluate an open-source tool with your own (non-identifiable) data before committing — de-risking the decision with something you can actually run.

  • PoC build & deployment of a shortlisted open tool in a sandbox you control
  • Integration spikes — connecting to FHIR, imaging, or data pipelines
  • Handover — documentation and a clear path to production (or a clear "not yet")

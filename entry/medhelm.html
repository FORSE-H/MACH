MedHELM Adopt

Comprehensive evaluation framework for benchmarking LLMs on medical tasks, built on Stanford CRFM's HELM platform. Covers 121 clinician-validated tasks across 5 domains — clinical decision support, clinical note generation, patient communication, medical research assistance, and administration/workflow — spanning 35 benchmarks (17 existing + 18 newly formulated). Uses an LLM-jury evaluation methodology with agreement rates comparable to clinician ratings. Includes a live public leaderboard at medhelm.org. Published in Nature Medicine 2025 by Stanford CRFM, Shah Lab, and Microsoft Health.

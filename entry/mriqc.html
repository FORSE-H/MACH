MRIQC Adopt

Automated MRI quality control tool that extracts image quality metrics (IQMs) and generates visual reports for structural (T1w, T2w) and functional brain MRI scans. Runs before any preprocessing as a first-pass quality gate. BIDS-compliant, containerised via Docker, and integrates with Nipype workflows using ANTs and AFNI. Produces no-reference IQMs without requiring a ground-truth reference scan. NIH-funded (NIBIB, NIMH, NINDS), maintained by the NiPreps Community. Apache-2.0, 363 stars, v24.0.2. Used alongside fMRIPrep in standard neuroimaging pipelines.

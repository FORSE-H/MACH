ESMC Adopt

State-of-the-art protein language model from EvolutionaryScale (Biohub) trained on ~2.8 billion protein sequences. Generates embeddings that capture structural and functional relationships across atomic to evolutionary scales. Serves as the foundational model for ESMFold2, a diffusion-based structure prediction system that matches or exceeds AlphaFold3 performance. ESMFold2 validated on five therapeutic targets, producing de novo minibinders and antibody-derived scFvs with nanomolar affinities and high hit rates. 160K+ HuggingFace downloads. MIT, v3.2.2, 2.7k GitHub stars. Candido et al. 2026.

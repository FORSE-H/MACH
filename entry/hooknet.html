HookNet Caution

Multi-resolution convolutional neural network for semantic segmentation of whole-slide histopathology images. Uses two parallel U-Net-style encoder-decoder branches operating at different magnifications (context and target), coupled via a 'hooking' mechanism that injects contextual feature maps into the detail branch. Demonstrated on multi-class tissue segmentation in breast cancer and TLS/germinal centre segmentation in lung cancer. MIT, TensorFlow 2.x, pip-installable. Published in Medical Image Analysis 2021. Developed by Diagnostic Image Analysis Group, Radboud University Medical Center.

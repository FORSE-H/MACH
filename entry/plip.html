The first vision-language foundation model for computational pathology — a CLIP-based contrastive model fine-tuned on 208,414 pathology image-text pairs sourced from medical Twitter posts. Enables zero-shot tissue and cancer classification from natural language prompts without task-specific training. Evaluated across multiple pathology classification tasks.

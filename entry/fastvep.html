fastVEP Assess

High-performance variant effect predictor written in Rust. Annotates genomic variants with consequence, impact, HGVS names, ClinVar, population frequency (gnomAD), AlphaMissense, and ACMG-AMP pathogenicity class (Richards 2015 + ClinGen SVI rule set). Inspired by and compatible with Ensembl VEP and Illumina Nirvana. Offers a CLI, a self-hostable REST API, and a browser-based annotator at fastvep.org. From the Huang Lab, Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai.

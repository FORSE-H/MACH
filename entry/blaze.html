Blaze Adopt

High-performance FHIR R4 server with an integrated CQL (Clinical Quality Language) evaluation engine, designed for population-wide aggregate queries across millions of patient records. Supports $evaluate-measure, $everything, $validate-code, terminology services (LOINC, SNOMED CT), and horizontal scalability via a distributed storage variant. Built in Clojure. Single-command Docker deployment. Extensively deployed within Germany's Medical Informatics Initiative (MII) and European biobank infrastructure. Apache-2.0, 219 stars, v1.7.0 (April 2026). Commercial support via KF Digital Health GmbH.

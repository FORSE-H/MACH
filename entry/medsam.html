MedSAM Assess

Universal medical image segmentation model adapting Meta's Segment Anything Model (SAM) to 30+ imaging modalities including CT, MRI, ultrasound, X-ray, endoscopy, and microscopy. Trained on over 1 million image-mask pairs across diverse anatomical structures. Requires only a bounding box prompt — no per-dataset retraining. Ships with LiteMedSAM (10× faster lightweight variant), a 3D Slicer plugin, and CLI/GUI/notebook inference interfaces. The de-facto open foundation for promptable medical image segmentation since the Nature Communications 2024 paper.

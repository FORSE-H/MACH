PLIP Assess

The first vision-language foundation model for computational pathology — a CLIP-based contrastive model fine-tuned on 208,414 pathology image-text pairs sourced from medical Twitter posts. Enables zero-shot tissue and cancer classification from natural language prompts without task-specific training. Evaluated across multiple pathology classification tasks. Published in Nature Medicine 2023. Model weights hosted on HuggingFace (vinid/plip), 75k+ monthly downloads. 376 stars. Research use only.
